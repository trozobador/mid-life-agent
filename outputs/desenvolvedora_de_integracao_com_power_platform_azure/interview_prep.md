## 6. Pre-Interview Checklist

- **Company Research:** Look up recent news, products, and the company's tech stack. Understand their approach to innovation and culture.
- **Role Research:** Review technical topics like Azure Logic Apps, Power Platform, and AI integration. Refresh knowledge on Python and Azure Functions.
- **Logistics:** Prepare a portfolio or code samples, have certifications ready to discuss, and ensure you have a reliable internet connection for the interview.

## 7. Salary & Negotiation Tips

- **Research Market Rates:** Understand the typical salary range for this role in your region, considering your experience and certifications.
- **Highlight Unique Skills:** Emphasize your unique blend of technical and leadership skills during negotiations.
- **Be Prepared to Discuss Benefits:** Consider total compensation, including benefits, when discussing salary expectations.
- **Negotiate with Confidence:** Be clear about your value and be prepared to justify your salary expectations with examples of past achievements and market data.
