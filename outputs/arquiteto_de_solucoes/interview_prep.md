## 1. Role Analysis

### Summary of the Role
The role of a "Arquiteto de Soluções" at FCamara requires a professional who can deeply understand business needs and translate them into robust, scalable, and secure architectural solutions that add value to the organization. The role emphasizes the need for strong business analysis capabilities, a deep understanding of architectural design, and the ability to document and present solutions effectively.

### Top 3 "Must-Haves"
1. **Integration Expertise**: Must have proven experience in integrating systems, especially using APIs and OAuth, which is crucial for developing scalable solutions.
2. **Architectural Design Skills**: Ability to choose appropriate architectural patterns (like microservices, message queues) and justify these choices based on business and technical requirements.
3. **Security and Performance Focus**: Experience in implementing security measures and ensuring high performance and scalability in solutions.

## 2. Likely Interview Questions & Suggested Answers

### Technical/Hard-Skill Questions

**Question 1**: Can you describe a project where you implemented a microservices architecture?
- **Why they ask**: To assess your experience with microservices and understanding of its benefits and challenges.
- **Suggested Answer**: At BlueCX, I led the integration of Copilot Studio with vector databases using a microservices architecture. This allowed us to scale individual components independently and improve system resilience. We utilized Docker and Kubernetes for deployment, ensuring high availability and performance.
- **Talking Points**: Microservices benefits, Docker/Kubernetes usage, scalability and resilience.

**Question 2**: How do you ensure the security of an integrated system?
- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your understanding of security protocols and practices.
- **Suggested Answer**: In my role at NTT DATA, I implemented OAuth for secure API integrations, ensuring that all data exchanges were encrypted and authenticated. This reduced security breaches and improved client trust.
- **Talking Points**: OAuth implementation, encryption, client trust.

**Question 3**: What tools do you use for system monitoring and observability?
- **Why they ask**: To understand your approach to maintaining system health and performance.
- **Suggested Answer**: I use Azure Monitor and Log Analytics for real-time monitoring and diagnostics. These tools help us track system performance and quickly respond to any anomalies, ensuring high availability.
- **Talking Points**: Azure Monitor, real-time diagnostics, high availability.

**Question 4**: Describe your experience with cloud platforms like Azure or AWS.
- **Why they ask**: To gauge your familiarity with cloud technologies.
- **Suggested Answer**: I have extensive experience with Azure, particularly in deploying AI solutions and integrating services like Azure AI Studio and Azure OpenAI. This has enabled us to leverage cloud scalability and reliability for our AI projects.
- **Talking Points**: Azure AI Studio, cloud scalability, AI integration.

**Question 5**: How do you approach performance optimization in your projects?
- **Why they ask**: To assess your ability to enhance system efficiency.
- **Suggested Answer**: At Algar Tech, I optimized our RPA tool by refining the back-end processes and using .NET Core, which improved response times by 30% and reduced server load.
- **Talking Points**: .NET Core optimization, server load reduction, efficiency gains.

- **Why they ask**: To assess your strategic planning and design skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: I start by understanding the client's business needs and existing infrastructure. Then, I map out functional domains, define business capabilities, and choose appropriate architectural patterns, ensuring alignment with business goals and technical constraints.
- **Talking Points**: Client needs assessment, architectural pattern selection, business alignment.

**Question 12**: What steps would you take to migrate a legacy system to a new architecture?
- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your migration planning and risk management skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: I would conduct a thorough analysis of the current system, identify dependencies, and develop a phased migration plan. This includes risk assessment and mitigation strategies to ensure a smooth transition.
- **Talking Points**: Dependency analysis, phased migration, risk mitigation.

**Question 13**: How would you handle a situation where a new technology you recommended is not performing as expected?
- **Why they ask**: To understand your problem-solving and adaptability.
- **Suggested Answer**: I would first analyze the root cause of the performance issues, consult with the team for insights, and explore alternative solutions or optimizations to address the problem effectively.
- **Talking Points**: Root cause analysis, team consultation, solution optimization.
