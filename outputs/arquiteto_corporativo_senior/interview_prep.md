## 2. Likely Interview Questions & Suggested Answers

### Technical/Hard-Skill Questions

1. **Question**: Can you describe your experience with microservices architecture?
   - **Why they ask**: To assess your practical knowledge and experience in implementing microservices, which is critical for the role.
   - **Suggested Answer**: "At NTT DATA, I led the implementation of microservices architecture for a large financial client. This approach improved system scalability and allowed for more flexible deployments. For instance, by using Azure Functions and Docker, we achieved a 30% reduction in deployment time."
   - **Talking Points**: Scalability improvements, specific technologies used (Azure Functions, Docker), impact on deployment time.

2. **Question**: How have you applied TOGAF in your previous projects?
   - **Why they ask**: To determine your familiarity and practical application of TOGAF in architectural projects.
   - **Suggested Answer**: "In my role at Sistema Educacional Brasileiro S.A., I utilized TOGAF to establish an enterprise architecture framework that aligned IT strategy with business goals. 3. **Question**: What design patterns do you frequently use in your architecture work?

   - **Talking Points**: Application of DDD principles, improved stakeholder communication, system adaptability.

5. **Question**: How do you ensure the security and scalability of your architecture solutions?
   - **Why they ask**: To understand your approach to balancing security with scalability in architectural design.
   - **Suggested Answer**: "Security and scalability are integral to my design philosophy.
