# Interview Preparation Guide for Ricardo Martins

## 1. Role Analysis

**Summary of the Role:**
MESHA Tecnologia is seeking a Desenvolvedor Power Platform who can design, develop, and implement applications using the Power Platform suite, including Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI. The role requires integrating these tools with other Microsoft solutions and external systems, ensuring data quality and security, and providing user support. The company values diversity, meritocracy, and talent development, aiming to create impactful and innovative solutions for its clients.

**Top 3 "Must-Haves":**
1. **Expertise in Power Platform Tools:** Demonstrated ability to work with Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI to create applications, automate workflows, and develop interactive dashboards.
2. **Integration Skills:** Experience in integrating Power Platform with Microsoft Dynamics 365, SharePoint, and external systems using Azure Logic Apps and Azure Functions.
3. **Data Management and Security:** Strong understanding of data governance, ensuring data integrity and security in developed solutions.
