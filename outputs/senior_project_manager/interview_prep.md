# Interview Preparation Guide for Ricardo Martins

---

## 1. Role Analysis

**Summary**: The company is seeking a highly experienced Senior Project Manager to lead a large-scale Application Modernization initiative, focusing on migrating approximately 70 legacy applications to modern containerized environments in AWS. The role demands strategic execution, strong governance, stakeholder management, and coordination of multidisciplinary teams to ensure end-to-end delivery in a complex corporate environment.

**Top 3 Must-Haves**:
1. Extensive experience in IT Project Management, particularly in large-scale application modernization and legacy system migration to AWS.
2. Strong governance skills, including financial management, risk mitigation, and adherence to PMO standards.
3. Proven ability to manage and coordinate multidisciplinary teams and stakeholders effectively.

---

## 2. Likely Interview Questions & Suggested Answers

### Technical/Hard-Skill Questions

**Question 1**: Can you describe your experience with AWS and containerization?

- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your technical expertise in cloud environments and containerization, which are crucial for the role.
- **Suggested Answer**: "At BlueCX, I automated the integration of Dynamics 365 with legacy systems using AWS services, ensuring security and scalability. I also have experience with Docker, which I used to containerize applications, improving deployment efficiency."
- **Talking Points**: AWS services, Docker usage, impact on deployment efficiency.

**Question 2**: How have you used Azure DevOps in your projects?

- **Why they ask**: To assess your familiarity with Azure DevOps for project management and execution tracking.
- **Suggested Answer**: "During my tenure at NTT DATA, I utilized Azure DevOps for managing CI/CD pipelines, which streamlined our development processes and ensured consistent delivery standards."
- **Talking Points**: CI/CD pipelines, process streamlining, delivery standards.

**Question 3**: What strategies do you use for risk management in large projects?

- **Why they ask**: To understand your approach to identifying, evaluating, and mitigating risks.
- **Suggested Answer**: "I conduct comprehensive risk assessments at the project's onset and implement mitigation plans. At SEB, I negotiated budgets to address potential financial risks proactively."
- **Talking Points**: Risk assessments, mitigation plans, budget negotiations.

**Question 4**: Describe your experience with governance and PMO standards.

- **Why they ask**: To gauge your ability to maintain project governance and adhere to PMO standards.
- **Suggested Answer**: "At Algar Tech, I established governance frameworks that aligned with PMO standards, which improved project transparency and compliance."
- **Talking Points**: Governance frameworks, PMO alignment, transparency.

**Question 5**: How do you manage project budgets and forecasts?

- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your financial management skills in project settings.
- **Suggested Answer**: "I use detailed forecasting and regular budget reviews to ensure financial control. This approach helped me reduce costs in the Dynamics 365 project at Algar Tech by 25%."
- **Talking Points**: Forecasting, budget reviews, cost reduction.

### Behavioral Questions

**Question 6**: Tell me about a time when you led a team through a challenging project.

- **Why they ask**: To assess your leadership and problem-solving skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: "At BlueCX, I led a team in implementing Dynamics 365's Marketing module, reducing bugs by 95%. I fostered collaboration and clear communication to overcome technical challenges."
- **Talking Points**: Leadership, collaboration, communication.

**Question 7**: Describe a situation where you had to manage multiple stakeholders with conflicting interests.

- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your stakeholder management skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: "At NTT DATA, I managed stakeholders from technical and non-technical backgrounds, aligning their interests by facilitating workshops and maintaining open communication."
- **Talking Points**: Stakeholder alignment, workshops, communication.

**Question 8**: Can you give an example of how you handled a project that was falling behind schedule?

- **Why they ask**: To understand your ability to manage project timelines.
- **Suggested Answer**: "During a project at SEB, I re-prioritized tasks and allocated additional resources, which brought the project back on track without compromising quality."
- **Talking Points**: Re-prioritization, resource allocation, quality maintenance.

**Question 9**: Tell me about a time you had to adapt to significant changes during a project.

- **Why they ask**: To assess your adaptability and flexibility.
- **Suggested Answer**: "At Adentis Portugal, a client requested a major feature change mid-project. I adapted by revising the project plan and reallocating resources, ensuring we met the new requirements."
- **Talking Points**: Adaptability, resource reallocation, meeting requirements.

**Question 10**: Describe a successful project you managed from start to finish.

- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your project management skills and ability to deliver results.
- **Suggested Answer**: "I led the revitalization of Algar Tech's BackOffice using Dynamics 365, which improved reliability by 40% and reduced future costs by 25%. My focus on stakeholder engagement and process optimization was key to success."
- **Talking Points**: Stakeholder engagement, process optimization, measurable outcomes.

### Strategic/Situational Questions

**Question 11**: How would you approach the migration of legacy systems to AWS?

- **Why they ask**: To understand your strategic planning and execution skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: "I would start with a thorough assessment of the existing systems, followed by a phased migration plan. Leveraging AWS's scalability, I would ensure minimal disruption and focus on training the team for new environments."
- **Talking Points**: System assessment, phased migration, team training.

**Question 12**: What would be your strategy for managing a multidisciplinary team?

- **Why they ask**: To evaluate your team management and coordination skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: "I would establish clear communication channels and define roles and responsibilities. Regular check-ins and fostering a collaborative environment are crucial for team success."
- **Talking Points**: Communication, role clarity, collaboration.

**Question 13**: How would you ensure compliance with PMO standards in a complex project?

- **Why they ask**: To assess your governance and compliance skills.
- **Suggested Answer**: "I would implement a governance framework that aligns with PMO standards, conduct regular audits, and ensure documentation is up-to-date to maintain compliance."
- **Talking Points**: Governance framework, audits, documentation.

### Motivation/Fit Questions

**Question 14**: Why are you interested in this Senior Project Manager role?

- **Why they ask**: To understand your motivation and fit for the role.
- **Suggested Answer**: "This role aligns with my expertise in application modernization and my passion for leading large-scale projects. I am excited about the opportunity to leverage my AWS and governance skills in a challenging environment."
- **Talking Points**: Alignment with expertise, passion for large-scale projects, AWS and governance skills.

**Question 15**: Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

- **Why they ask**: To gauge your career aspirations and long-term fit with the company.
- **Suggested Answer**: "In 5 years, I aim to be a thought leader in IT project management, driving innovation and efficiency in application modernization projects. I see myself growing within this company, contributing to its strategic goals."

## 4. Potential Red Flags to Address Proactively

1. **Limited Direct Experience with AWS**: While you have AWS experience, ensure you emphasize any relevant projects to counteract any perceived gaps.
   - **Proactive Approach**: Highlight specific AWS projects and any training or certifications.

2. **Transition from Technical to Strategic Role**: Your background is heavily technical; you need to demonstrate your strategic and leadership capabilities.
   - **Proactive Approach**: Focus on leadership roles and strategic decisions in your projects.

3. **Lack of Formal Project Management Certification**: If lacking PMP or similar certifications, emphasize your practical experience and successful project outcomes.
   - **Proactive Approach**: Discuss your governance frameworks and project management methodologies.

## 5. Questions to Ask the Interviewer

1. Can you describe the team structure and the key stakeholders for this project?
2. What are the biggest challenges you foresee in this application modernization initiative?
3. How does the company measure success for this role and project?
4. What is the company's approach to innovation and continuous improvement?
5. How does the company support professional development and career growth?
6. Can you provide more details about the company's culture and values?
7. What are the expectations for the first 90 days in this role?
8. How does the company handle risk management and compliance in projects?
9. What tools and technologies are currently being used for project management?
10. How does the company ensure alignment between IT and business objectives?

## 6. Pre-Interview Checklist

- **Company Research**: Look up the company's recent news, products, and culture. Understand their approach to technology and innovation.
- **Role Research**: Review AWS, containerization, and governance frameworks. Refresh knowledge on PMO standards and stakeholder management.
- **Logistics**: Prepare your portfolio, code samples, and case studies. Ensure you can discuss your certifications and relevant experiences.

## 7. Salary & Negotiation Tips

- **Salary Expectations**: Research the market rate for Senior Project Managers in your region, considering your extensive experience and expertise.
- **Negotiation Tips**: Be prepared to discuss your value, focusing on your leadership in large-scale projects and technical skills. Highlight your unique contributions to past projects and readiness to lead complex initiatives.
